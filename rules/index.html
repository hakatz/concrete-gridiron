Waivers & Free Agency (FAAB)

  • Free Agent Acquisition Budget (FAAB): $100 per season
  • Waiver claims processed: Wednesday morning
  • After waivers clear, remaining free agents may be added immediately (no bid)
  • Dropped players go on waivers for 2 days before becoming free agents
  • No FAAB rollover between seasons

Playoffs

  • Regular season: 14 weeks (Weeks 1-14)
  • Playoffs: Weeks 15-17 (3 rounds)
  • 6 teams qualify (top 6 by record, then PF as tiebreaker)
  • Week 15: Seeds 3v6, 4v5 (first round byes for seeds 1-2)
  • Week 16: Semifinals
  • Week 17: Championship

Last Place Punishment

The manager finishing in last place (12th) earns the last place punishment — a rotating punishment determined by a vote of all league members before the following season. Past punishments have included eating disgusting food combinations on video, posting embarrassing photos, and public pledges.

The shame lives in the record books forever.
